Reaction experiment

Neon Catch

How to play

  1. Press Start round. A glowing circle appears in the play area.
  2. Click or tap the circle before it jumps. Each hit gives you 10 points.
  3. Clicking empty space counts as a miss, so aim carefully.
  4. After 20 seconds you see your score, accuracy and best streak.

The circle jumps at the same pace all round. That keeps the result about steadiness, not about a difficulty curve that keeps rising.

Decision experiment

Human Decision Machine

What happens

You will see six short situations. Each one has four possible ways to respond, and none of them is correct. After your sixth choice, the machine shows which habits appeared most often and what that combination looks like.

  1. Read the situation.
  2. Pick the option closest to what you would really do.
  3. Read the short comment, then move on.
  4. Get your habit profile at the end.
