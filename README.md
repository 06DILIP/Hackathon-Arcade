# 🕹️ Hackathon Arcade

> Two short experiments about how people respond: one tests how quickly you can track a moving target, the other looks at how you choose when there is no right answer.

**🔗 Live demo:** [06dilip.github.io/Hackathon-Arcade](https://06dilip.github.io/Hackathon-Arcade/)

An individual hackathon collection built in 2026. Every project in the arcade is based on one of two ideas: **reflexes** or **reasoning**.

---

## 📊 At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **8** | Projects in the arcade |
| **2** | Different experiments |
| **30** | Decision scenarios |
| **≤ 2 min** | Per run |

The arcade has seven reaction projects and one decision project.

---

## 🎮 The Experiments

### ⚡ Neon Catch (Reflexes)

Follow a glowing circle for 20 seconds and click it before it jumps. The speed never changes, so your score shows how *steady* you stay, not how you cope with a speed-up.

- 🎯 10 points for every hit
- ❌ Clicking empty space counts as a miss
- 💾 Your best score is saved on your device
- 📈 Results show your score and accuracy

### 🧠 Human Decision Machine (Reasoning)

Face six everyday situations with four ways to respond to each. There are no right answers. The machine counts which of seven habits you used most and explains the pattern.

- 🎲 Six random situations every run, drawn from 30 scenarios
- 🧩 Seven habits tracked, from risk-taking to adaptability
- 📝 Ends with a profile and a list of your choices

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Choose a project.** Each card opens one of the two experiments.
2. **Play it through.** Only what the game needs is recorded: your clicks, or the options you pick.
3. **Read your result.** You get a score and accuracy from Neon Catch, or a habit profile from the Decision Machine.

---

## 🔒 Privacy

Nothing leaves your browser. There is no backend, no account and no tracking. Your best Neon Catch score is stored locally on your device.
